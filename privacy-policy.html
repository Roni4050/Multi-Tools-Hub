Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective Date: November 19, 2026

1. Overview and Core Privacy Commitment

At MultiTools Hub ("we", "us", or "our"), available at https://multitools-hub.web.app, user privacy is not an afterthought—it is the foundational design pillar of our entire software architecture. We believe that your sensitive documents, personal photographs, commercial stock illustrations, and video assets belong to you alone.

This Privacy Policy outlines how MultiTools Hub operates, detailing the zero-transmission nature of our client-side processing tools, the minimal technical data collected for hosting reliability, and our adherence to international privacy regulations including GDPR and CCPA.

2. 100% Client-Side Processing (Zero File Uploads)

Key Fact: When you use our PDF Merger, PDF Splitter, Image Resizer, WebP Converter, EXIF Cleaner, Safe Zone Checker, or QR Code Generator, your files never leave your device.

All document and media manipulations take place entirely within your browser session using web standards including:

  • FileReader API: Reads your local files directly into memory buffers within your browser.
  • HTML5 Canvas API: Performs pixel-level resizing, format conversion, watermark rendering, and EXIF sanitization locally.
  • Client-Side JavaScript Engines: Libraries such as pdf-lib and JSZip execute strictly within client memory.

Because files are never transmitted to our servers, we cannot see, store, copy, inspect, or share your documents under any circumstances.

3. LocalStorage and User Preferences

MultiTools Hub uses the standard web browser localStorage API for a single user-centric purpose: storing your Dark Mode / Light Mode theme preference. This value is stored locally on your device and is never synchronized to any remote database. You can clear this at any time by wiping your browser's site data.

4. Hosting Infrastructure and Server Logs

MultiTools Hub is statically hosted on Google Firebase Hosting. When your browser requests static assets (such as HTML, CSS, or JS files), Firebase servers automatically log standard network metadata:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Date, time, and requested URL path
  • HTTP referrer headers and response status codes

This data is aggregated strictly for security maintenance, DDoS protection, traffic diagnostic purposes, and CDN cache optimization. It is never linked to personally identifiable information.

5. Cookies and Google AdSense Advertising

To keep our utility suite 100% free for all users worldwide, MultiTools Hub may display contextual advertising powered by Google AdSense and third-party advertising networks.

  • Google Cookies: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on MultiTools Hub. Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and other sites across the Internet.
  • Opt-Out Options: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

6. Third-Party Content Delivery Networks (CDNs)

To deliver rapid loading speeds globally, our static site loads open-source libraries from public CDNs (including Tailwind CDN, Lucide Icons, jsDelivr, and unpkg). When your browser downloads these script files, your IP address is visible to the respective CDN provider solely for HTTP asset delivery.

7. Rights Under GDPR & CCPA

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users possess specific rights regarding personal data:

  • Right to Know / Access: You have the right to request what personal information is processed. Because MultiTools Hub does not collect or retain personal user files, no personal data repository exists.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: Any local preference data can be deleted instantly by clearing your browser cache and cookies.
  • Non-Discrimination: We do not discriminate against any user exercising their statutory privacy rights.

8. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

MultiTools Hub is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
