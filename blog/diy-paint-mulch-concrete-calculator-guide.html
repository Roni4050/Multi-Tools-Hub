Interior & Exterior Wall Paint Math (Standard 350 Sq Ft Rule)

As a universal rule of thumb across major paint manufacturers (Sherwin-Williams, Benjamin Moore, Behr), one US gallon of paint covers approximately 350 to 400 square feet of smooth wall space with one coat.

The Wall Area Calculation Formula:

  1. Calculate Perimeter: 2 × (Length + Width)
  2. Calculate Gross Wall Area: Perimeter × Ceiling Height
  3. Deduct Openings: Subtract 21 sq ft per standard interior door and 15 sq ft per standard window.
  4. Multiply by Coats: Standard painting requires 2 coats for rich, uniform color saturation.
  5. Divide by 350: Divide total paintable area by 350 to determine exact gallons needed, rounding up to the nearest whole gallon.

Landscaping Mulch & Garden Soil: Converting Sq Ft to Cubic Yards

Retail landscaping mulch at garden centers is sold in 2 cubic foot bags (or 3 cu. ft. contractor bags), whereas bulk landscaping delivery is sold by the Cubic Yard (27 cubic feet).

How to Calculate Mulch Needed:

Multiply garden bed length by width to get square footage. Multiply by desired depth in feet (a recommended 3-inch depth = 3/12 = 0.25 feet). Divide the total cubic feet by 27 to find cubic yards, or divide by 2 to determine how many standard 2-cubic-foot retail bags you need to load into your vehicle.

Concrete Slab Pouring: Bags vs. Ready-Mix Delivery Trucks

When pouring a concrete slab for a patio, walkway, or trash can pad, standard residential thickness is 4 inches (0.333 feet). To calculate concrete volume in cubic yards:

Cubic Yards = (Length in ft × Width in ft × (Thickness in inches / 12)) / 27 × 1.10 (10% waste buffer)

Bag Yield Rules:

  • One 80 lb bag of Quikrete yields approx. 0.60 cubic feet (45 bags make 1 cubic yard).
  • One 60 lb bag of Quikrete yields approx. 0.45 cubic feet (60 bags make 1 cubic yard).

Golden Rule: If your project requires more than 3.5 cubic yards (approx. 160 bags of 80lb mix), do not mix bags by hand! Order a local ready-mix concrete transit truck.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I add a 10% safety buffer to concrete and mulch calculations?
Ground excavations and garden soil beds are never laser-flat. Sub-base depressions, forms bowing under wet concrete pressure, and material spillage during mixing mean ordering exact mathematical minimums will leave you short right before the finish.
How many bags of 2 cu. ft. mulch fit in a standard passenger sedan?
A standard midsize sedan trunk comfortably holds 4 to 6 bags of 2 cu. ft. mulch. A crossover SUV with the rear seats folded can hold 12 to 16 bags. If your project requires 30+ bags, paying for home delivery or renting a $19 truck at Home Depot is much safer for vehicle suspension.
How thick should a concrete driveway be for vehicles?
Standard passenger car driveways should be at least 4 inches thick on a compacted gravel base. For heavy pickup trucks, RVs, or delivery vans, a 5-inch or 6-inch slab reinforced with #4 steel rebar or wire mesh is strongly recommended.
Does unprimed fresh drywall require extra paint?
Yes. Fresh gypsum drywall and joint compound (mud) are highly porous and soak up paint like a sponge. Always apply one coat of dedicated PVA drywall primer before applying your two finish coats of colored paint.
