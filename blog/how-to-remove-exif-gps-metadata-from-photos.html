What Hidden Information Is Stored Inside EXIF Headers?

A standard smartphone photograph embeds dozens of invisible metadata tags:

  • GPS Coordinates: Exact latitude, longitude, and altitude identifying where the photo was taken (such as your home, workplace, or child school).
  • Timestamp: Date and time down to the second.
  • Hardware Identifiers: Phone brand, lens focal length, aperture, shutter speed, and device serial numbers.
  • Software Footprint: Operating system build and editing application history.
