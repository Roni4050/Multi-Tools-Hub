Standard American Business Card Dimensions & 10-Up Layouts

The standard American business card measures exactly 3.5 inches wide by 2.0 inches high (88.9 mm × 50.8 mm). This format fits perfectly inside standard US wallets, cardholders, and Rolodex sleeves. (Note that international European business cards are slightly larger at 85 mm × 55 mm).

To print cards efficiently at home, standard 8.5 × 11 inch US Letter cardstock is arranged in a 10-Up Grid (2 columns wide by 5 rows tall):

  • Sheet Dimensions: 8.5 in × 11.0 in (612 × 792 PDF points)
  • Card Dimensions: 3.5 in × 2.0 in (252 × 144 PDF points)
  • Side Margins: 0.75 in (54 pt) Left and Right
  • Top/Bottom Margins: 0.5 in (36 pt) Top and Bottom
  • Gaps: Zero gap between cards; cut along precise crop marks

Choosing the Right Paper Stock: GSM and Cover Weights

The single factor that distinguishes a luxury business card from a flimsy home printout is paper weight. Standard copy paper is 20 lb bond (75 gsm) and is completely unsuitable for cards. Instead, look for:

  • 80 lb Cover (216 gsm): The minimum acceptable cardstock for home printing. Feeds reliably through standard desktop paper trays.
  • 100 lb to 110 lb Cover (270–300 gsm): The industry sweet spot. Feels rigid, premium, and durable. Most modern laser printers handle this weight with a manual bypass tray.
  • Pre-Perforated Sheets (Avery 5371 / 8371): Micro-perforated cardstock allows you to punch out individual cards cleanly without requiring a guillotine paper cutter.

Designing Professional Corporate Letterhead on US Letter (8.5x11")

Unlike Europe which uses A4 paper (210 × 297 mm), the United States exclusively uses US Letter (8.5 × 11 inches). When designing corporate letterhead, adhere to the 1-inch rule:

  • Header Zone: Reserve the top 1.25 inches for your company logo, legal entity name, and contact details.
  • Body Margins: Maintain 0.75-inch left and right margins for letter correspondence text.
  • Footer Zone: Dedicate the bottom 0.75 inch to confidentiality statements, registration numbers, or website URLs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What printer settings should I select to avoid crooked or scaled cards?
Always select "Actual Size" or set Page Scaling to "100%" in your Adobe Acrobat or browser print dialog. Never select "Fit to Printable Area", as shrinking the PDF even by 3% will misalign the cards with pre-perforated Avery sheet lines.
What is the difference between Avery 5371 and Avery 8371?
Avery 5371 is formulated specifically for desktop Laser printers, whereas Avery 8371 is engineered with quick-drying coating for Inkjet printers. Using inkjet paper in a high-heat laser printer can cause jams and smudging.
Should business cards be printed with glossy or matte finishes?
Matte or smooth satin finish is strongly recommended for professional services (law, finance, consulting, healthcare) because it allows clients to write notes or cell numbers on the card. High-gloss finishes are better suited for photographers and visual artists.
Are cutting crop marks necessary when using pre-perforated paper?
Crop marks are not strictly necessary on pre-perforated paper, but they serve as essential visual guides to verify that your printer's mechanical feed rollers fed the paper squarely without horizontal skew.
