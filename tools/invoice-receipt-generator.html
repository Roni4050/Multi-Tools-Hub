How to Generate Compliant Invoices and Receipts for US Businesses

Whether you operate as an independent contractor (1099 freelancer), an LLC owner, or an e-commerce seller, maintaining clear, professional invoices and payment receipts is essential for cash flow, legal client relationships, and IRS tax compliance. This guide outlines standard practices for US business documentation.

Key Elements Required on Every US Business Invoice

  • Legal Business Information: Your registered trade name (DBA or LLC), street address, state, ZIP code, contact telephone, and billing email.
  • Unique Sequential Invoice Number: Essential for your bookkeeping and your client's accounts payable system (e.g. INV-2026-001).
  • Issue Date & Due Date: Prevents ambiguities regarding payment windows (such as Net 14, Net 30, or Due on Receipt).
  • Itemized Breakdown: Individual line items detailing the service or product delivered, quantity/hours, unit price, and subtotal.
  • Applicable Sales Tax: Calculated on taxable goods or services based on your local or destination state tax jurisdiction.
