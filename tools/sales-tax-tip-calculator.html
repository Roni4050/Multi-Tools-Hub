US Restaurant Tipping Etiquette & Best Practices

Unlike European and Asian countries where hospitality service charges are bundled directly into the menu pricing, the United States relies heavily on voluntary gratuity. In standard US restaurants, servers frequently earn a sub-minimum tipped wage (as low as $2.13/hour under federal law), meaning your tips constitute the majority of their actual income.

Standard US Tipping Benchmarks

15% - Acceptable Baseline Standard gratuity for polite, average service without notable extras.
18% to 20% - US Standard The expected benchmark for good, attentive sit-down table service in modern American dining.
22% to 25% - Exceptional Reserved for fine dining, large parties with complex orders, or holiday hospitality.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I tip before or after sales tax?

Etiquette experts agree that tipping on the pre-tax subtotal is traditional, as sales tax is paid directly to the government, not the service staff. However, tipping on the post-tax total has become widespread with automated handheld payment terminals. Our calculator supports both.

What is automatic gratuity (Auto-Grat)?

Many US restaurants automatically apply an 18% or 20% service fee for parties of 6 or more guests. Always inspect your itemized check before adding an additional tip so you do not accidentally double-tip!
