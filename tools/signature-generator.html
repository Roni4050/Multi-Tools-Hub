Legal Validity and Best Practices for Digital Signatures

In an increasingly remote and digital business environment, signing documents online has transitioned from a convenience to a necessity. Whether executing employment agreements, non-disclosure contracts, or mortgage documents, having an authentic transparent PNG and vector SVG signature ensures smooth paperless workflows.

Understanding the ESIGN Act and UETA Regulations

In the United States, the legal validity of electronic signatures is enshrined under two landmark statutes:

  • The ESIGN Act (2000): A federal law establishing that electronic signatures and electronic records have the same legal standing as ink-on-paper signatures in interstate and global commerce.
  • The Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (UETA): Adopted by 49 states, certifying that transactions conducted electronically cannot be denied enforceability solely because an electronic format was utilized.

Transparent PNG vs. Vector SVG: Which Should You Use?

Transparent PNG (High-Res 1200x400)

Best for pasting directly into Word documents, Google Docs, Apple Pages, email signatures, or dragging into Adobe Acrobat PDF signing fields. It has zero background and blends onto any document paper color.

Scalable Vector SVG

Best for graphic designers, web developers, and large print documentation. SVG stores the mathematical curves of your stroke, allowing it to scale up to billboard size without any pixelation or blurriness.
