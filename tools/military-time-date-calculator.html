Why 24-Hour Time is Mandated in Essential US Industries

In healthcare, aviation, cybersecurity, and the military, mistaking morning for evening can lead to lethal medication overdoses or flight dispatch failures. By numbering each hour consecutively from 0000 to 2359, 24-hour time ensures instantaneous precision without reliance on AM/PM suffixes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is midnight 2400 or 0000?

Both are used depending on context: 2400 designates the exact end of a calendar day, while 0000 designates the beginning of the new calendar day.
