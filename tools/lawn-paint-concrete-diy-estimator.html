Standard US Material Calculations for DIYers

When taking on home improvement projects in the United States, materials sold at national retailers like The Home Depot, Lowe's, and Menards are packaged in standard US customary increments. Knowing how to convert square feet to cubic yards or paint gallons saves homeowners hundreds of dollars in excess trips and unneeded overstock.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I order a concrete ready-mix truck instead of mixing bags?

If your project requires more than 3 to 4 cubic yards (approx. 135 to 180 bags of 80lb concrete), hand-mixing with a portable mixer becomes physically exhausting and risks cold joints (uneven curing). Concrete transit-mix delivery trucks are much faster, more structurally uniform, and often cost-effective for large slabs.
