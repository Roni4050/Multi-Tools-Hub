Understanding Color Codes: HEX, RGB, and HSL

HEX (#RRGGBB)

Hexadecimal color representation is standard in CSS web design, Figma, Photoshop, and Illustrator stylesheets.

RGB (Red, Green, Blue)

Additive color values (0-255) ideal for digital screens, video editors, and canvas manipulation.

HSL (Hue, Saturation, Lightness)

Human-intuitive color format allowing you to create darker shades or lighter tints simply by adjusting the lightness percentage.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the color extraction algorithm find the dominant swatches?

The tool samples thousands of pixels across an HTML5 Canvas and quantizes them into harmonic color clusters. It sorts by frequency while filtering out near-duplicate hues to provide 8 distinct, balanced tones.

Can I paste an image directly from my clipboard?

Yes! You can press Ctrl+V (or Cmd+V) anywhere on the page to paste a screenshot directly into the extractor without having to save it to your hard drive first.

How does the 'Use in SVG Editor' integration work?

Clicking the 'Use these colors in SVG Editor' button forwards your extracted HEX values directly to our SVG Color Changer via URL parameters, making them instantly available as quick color chips.

Are my images transmitted or saved to a server?

No. Pixel sampling and color quantization occur 100% locally in your browser memory.
