How Real Estate Commissions Function in the United States

In US real estate, real estate agents operate as independent 1099 contractors sponsored by a licensed broker. Although closing settlement statements (ALTA/CD) show a lump sum commission, that money is split multiple times: between the listing and buyer brokerages, between the managing broker and the agent, through franchise royalties (e.g. Keller Williams, Coldwell Banker), and finally through federal and state self-employment tax liabilities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do real estate agents set aside 25% to 30% for taxes?

Because real estate agents receive gross 1099 income with zero tax withholding, they are responsible for both the employer and employee portions of FICA (15.3% Self-Employment Tax for Social Security and Medicare) plus ordinary federal and state income taxes.
