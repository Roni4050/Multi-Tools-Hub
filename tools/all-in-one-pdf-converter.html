Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are my confidential PDF documents uploaded to a remote server?

No. MultiTools Hub executes all PDF processing, image rasterization, and text extraction 100% inside your client-side browser using WebAssembly, HTML5 Canvas, and Mozilla PDF.js. Your files never touch external servers or cloud storage.

How does vector SVG conversion maintain sharpness in the generated PDF?

When SVG files are added, our studio parses their vector paths and rasterizes them on high-DPI virtual canvases at double and triple device pixel ratios before compiling them into standard PDF containers, preserving crisp lines, typography, and contrast.

Can I convert multiple PDF pages to individual images at once?

Yes. Tab 2 renders every page of your uploaded PDF document into real-time visual previews. You can choose from JPG, PNG, WebP, or SVG output formats, adjust resolution scale (1x, 2x, 3x), and download individual pages or batch export all pages inside a zipped archive.

Does the text extractor preserve document layout and page breaks?

The PDF to Text and Markdown Extractor reconstructs continuous text streams while inserting structured markdown headers and horizontal separators for each page, making it effortless to copy into Obsidian, Notion, or plain text editors.
