Amazon KDP Cover Specifications and Spine Width Formula

Publishing a paperback or hardcover book through Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) requires preparing a single, continuous flat cover file that incorporates the Back Cover, Spine, Front Cover, and 0.125-inch Bleed on all four sides.

The single most frequent reason Amazon rejects cover PDF uploads is an improperly calculated spine width or missing bleed margin. MultiTools Hub applies the precise KDP mathematical formula to calculate your dimensions down to thousandths of an inch and exact 300 DPI pixels.

The Exact Math Behind KDP Paperback Covers

Spine Width Formula: Page Count × Paper Caliper Thickness

• White Paper: Page Count × 0.002252"

• Cream Paper: Page Count × 0.002500"

• Color Paper: Page Count × 0.002347"

Total Full Cover Width: 0.125" (Bleed) + Trim Width + Spine Width + Trim Width + 0.125" (Bleed)

Total Full Cover Height: 0.125" (Bleed) + Trim Height + 0.125" (Bleed)

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How is Amazon KDP spine width calculated?

Spine width equals your book's total page count multiplied by the paper caliper thickness: 0.002252 inches for standard white paper, 0.002500 inches for cream paper, and 0.002347 inches for color paper.

What is the standard bleed requirement for KDP paperbacks and hardcovers?

Amazon KDP requires an industry standard bleed of 0.125 inches (1/8th of an inch) on the top, bottom, and outside edges of your cover artwork to prevent white margins during mechanical trimming.

Why must my print cover design be at 300 DPI?

Commercial book printing requires 300 Dots Per Inch (DPI) to ensure high-definition typography, sharp barcode scanability, and vibrant photographic reproduction without pixelation.

Where must the barcode be placed on the back cover?

The barcode requires a reserved box of 2.0 inches wide by 1.2 inches high placed in the lower section of your back cover, set at least 0.25 inches away from all trim and spine fold lines.
