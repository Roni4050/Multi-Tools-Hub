Official Guide to US Passport and Visa Photo Specifications

Every year, over 200,000 US passport applications are delayed or rejected because submitted photos fail to meet the rigorous biometric criteria mandated by the US Department of State. Taking your own compliant passport photo at home is simple, fast, and completely free when you follow the exact dimensions and positioning guidelines below.

Official US Passport Photo Requirements (State Department Checklist)

Dimensions & Resolution

  • Size: Exactly 2 x 2 inches (51 x 51 mm)
  • Resolution: 600 x 600 pixels minimum at 300 DPI
  • Head Height: 1 inch to 1 3/8 inches (50% to 69% of height)
  • Eye Height: 1 1/8 inches to 1 3/8 inches from bottom

Lighting, Pose & Attire

  • Background: Solid plain white or off-white with zero shadows
  • Expression: Neutral facial expression, mouth closed
  • Eyeglasses: Strictly prohibited (no prescription or sunglasses)
  • Clothing: Normal daily clothing (uniforms or camouflage forbidden)

How to Print for Under $0.50 at Walgreens, CVS, or Walmart

Retail pharmacies charge upwards of $16.99 for two passport photos taken at their photo counter. However, all photo kiosks print standard 4x6 inch photographic prints on genuine glossy or matte photo paper for approximately $0.35 to $0.40.

  1. Upload your portrait to MultiTools Hub and align your eyes with the red guideline and head within the oval.
  2. Click Download 4x6" Print Sheet to receive a single 1200x1800 px image containing six 2x2 photos with scissor guidelines.
  3. Upload the downloaded 4x6 image file to the Walgreens, CVS, or Walmart Photo website as a standard 4x6 print (do not select their "passport photo" service).
  4. Pick up your 4x6 print in store for under 50 cents, cut out two photos along the dashed lines, and attach them to your DS-11 or DS-82 passport application.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use this photo for a US Visa or Green Card application?

Yes. The US Department of State and USCIS use the exact same 2x2 inch (600x600 px at 300 DPI) biometric standard for US Tourist Visas (B1/B2), Student Visas (F1), Diversity Visa Lotteries (DV Lottery), Green Card renewals (Form I-90), and US Naturalization Citizenship (Form N-400).

Can I take my passport photo with a smartphone?

Yes! Modern smartphones have cameras with more than enough megapixels to exceed the 300 DPI requirement. Have a family member or friend take your photo from 4 to 6 feet away in natural, indirect daylight in front of a white wall. Avoid selfies as front-facing cameras distort facial proportions.

Are my biometric facial photos stored or uploaded?

Never. MultiTools Hub is built on 100% client-side technology. All zooming, rotation, and photo sheet generation runs in your browser's local memory via the HTML5 Canvas API. Nothing is transmitted over the internet.
