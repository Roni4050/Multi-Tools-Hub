How the NHTSA Modulo 11 VIN Check Digit Works

In 1981, the United States National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) standardized all road motor vehicle identification numbers to exactly 17 characters. Position 9 contains a mathematical check digit calculated using a weighted modulus 11 formula. If someone attempts to forge a stolen car's VIN or alters a title certificate by swapping even a single character, the check digit will fail validation immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I find the VIN on my vehicle?

The most accessible location is on the driver's side dashboard visible through the lower windshield glass from outside the vehicle. It is also stamped on the driver's side door jamb certification label, your vehicle registration card, and your auto insurance card.
