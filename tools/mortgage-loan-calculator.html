How to Accurately Estimate Your True Monthly Mortgage Cost

Many first-time homebuyers only calculate their principal and interest payment, only to be surprised by an additional $400 to $900 per month in property taxes, hazard insurance, and PMI fees rolled into their lender's monthly escrow account. Understanding the full breakdown empowers you to shop confidently and negotiate better rates.

How Down Payments Directly Affect Your Monthly Payment

Putting down 20% achieves two major financial advantages:

  1. Eliminates PMI: You save between $100 and $250 every month by not having to pay Private Mortgage Insurance.
  2. Reduces Principal: A smaller borrowed amount results in substantially lower total interest over a 15 or 30-year amortization schedule.
