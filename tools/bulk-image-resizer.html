Choosing the Right Resize Mode: Fit vs. Crop vs. Stretch

Fit (Contain)

Scales the entire image down so that both dimensions fit within your target boundaries. Perfect for preserving complete product photographs or artwork without losing edge details.

Crop / Fill (Center Crop)

Fills the exact canvas dimensions and crops excess pixels from the sides or top/bottom. Recommended for creating uniform social media profile grids, thumbnail collections, or blog card headers.

Stretch

Forces the image directly into the specified width and height. Use with caution as this alters the original aspect ratio and may distort human faces or circular shapes.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I resize images while keeping their original proportions?

Yes! The Maintain Aspect Ratio checkbox automatically calculates the corresponding height whenever you change the width (and vice-versa), ensuring your images never get distorted or stretched.

What is the difference between Fit and Crop/Fill modes?

Fit (Contain) scales the entire image down to fit inside your target dimensions without cropping any content. Crop/Fill fills the exact dimensions and centers the crop so there are no empty borders.

Can I convert the file format while resizing?

Yes. You can keep the original format or convert your entire batch to WebP, JPEG, or PNG, and adjust the compression quality slider to control final file weight.

Are my images uploaded to any remote server?

Never. All image rendering, scaling algorithms, and ZIP packaging occur 100% inside your browser using HTML5 Canvas and JSZip.
