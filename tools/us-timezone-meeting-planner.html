US Time Zone Primer for Remote Workers & Executives

The continental United States spans four principal time zones across approximately 3,000 miles. When scheduling conference calls, client presentations, or webinars, coordinating across these three hours of differential is crucial to ensure nobody is dialed in outside reasonable working hours.

The Four Continental Zones at a Glance

Eastern Time (ET - UTC-5 / UTC-4) Home to New York, Washington D.C., Boston, and Miami. Sets the financial opening bell (9:30 AM ET).
Central Time (CT - UTC-6 / UTC-5) One hour behind Eastern. Major hubs include Chicago, Dallas, Houston, and Minneapolis.
Mountain Time (MT - UTC-7 / UTC-6) Two hours behind Eastern. Denver, Salt Lake City, and Phoenix (note: Arizona does not observe DST).
Pacific Time (PT - UTC-8 / UTC-7) Three hours behind Eastern. Tech & entertainment centers: Silicon Valley, Seattle, Los Angeles.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the best overlap window for coast-to-coast US teams?

Between 2:00 PM and 5:00 PM Eastern Time. During this exact 3-hour window, Pacific Coast teams have started their workday (11:00 AM - 2:00 PM PT), Central teams are in the afternoon (1:00 PM - 4:00 PM CT), and Mountain teams are midway (12:00 PM - 3:00 PM MT).
