How Weighted Grading Works in US High Schools & Universities

In US collegiate courses, grades are rarely determined by raw points alone. Instead, professors assign weights (percentages) to different coursework categories (e.g., Homework 20%, Quizzes 20%, Midterm 25%, and Final Exam 35%). Knowing the exact weight of your final allows you to budget your study time strategically.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a passing grade in US universities?

For general undergraduate electives, a "D" (60-69%) or "C" (70-72%) is technically passing. However, major prerequisite courses typically require a minimum grade of "C" (2.0 GPA) to advance to the next level.
