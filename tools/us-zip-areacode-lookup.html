History & Anatomy of the North American Numbering Plan (NANP)

In 1947, AT&T and the Bell System introduced the North American Numbering Plan (NANP) to automate long-distance telephone calling. Under this system, the most populous cities received area codes with the fastest rotary dial times—hence 212 was assigned to New York City, 213 to Los Angeles, and 312 to Chicago.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can an unknown phone call with a local area code be spoofed?

Yes. Robocallers frequently execute "neighbor spoofing," falsifying caller ID to display your local 3-digit area code so you are more likely to answer. Always verify unfamiliar numbers with an official directory.
