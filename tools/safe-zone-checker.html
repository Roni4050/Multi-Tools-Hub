Mastering Mobile Safe Zones for Shorts, Reels, and TikTok

In short-form vertical video (9:16 ratio, 1080×1920 pixels), visual competition is fierce. One of the most common pitfalls creator editors face is placing crucial subtitles, product callouts, or key visual subjects in zones where native social media interface elements overlay them.

When like counts, share arrows, caption accordions, or audio discs obscure your text, viewer retention drops immediately. MultiTools Hub provides an interactive safe-zone inspector that replicates real mobile UI coordinates for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok in real-time.

Platform Safe Zone Breakdown (1080 × 1920 px)

YouTube Shorts Margins

Keep critical text between Y: 240px and Y: 1440px. The top 240px contains camera/search controls, while the bottom 480px holds your channel badge, subscribe button, and sound info. The right-hand 180px margin holds the engagement stack.

Instagram Reels Margins

Reels overlays a 2-3 line caption zone at the bottom (Y: 1420px to 1920px). The top 220px is reserved for header controls, and the right 160px houses the heart, comment, and send icons. Keep subjects centrally focused.

TikTok Margins

The top header (LIVE, Following, For You) covers 0 to 200px. The bottom caption and spinning sound disc take up 460px. Placing focal graphics in the center 1080×1080 box guarantees 100% visibility across all phone form factors.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What are safe zones in vertical 9:16 short-form video?

Safe zones are the unobstructed viewable areas of a 9:16 screen that are free from native platform UI elements such as like buttons, comments, captions, sound titles, and header navigation.

Why do captions and important text get cut off in Shorts and Reels?

The bottom 20% to 25% of mobile screens are occupied by channel handles, captions, audio tags, and system navigation bars. Any text placed in this zone becomes obscured by the platform interface.

Can I download the preview with the safe zone overlay included?

Yes! Clicking 'Download Composite Preview' generates a high-resolution PNG with the exact overlay guides and UI indicators, perfect for sharing with clients or editing teams.

Does this tool upload my proprietary video frames to any server?

No. The entire rendering, scaling, overlay drawing, and image export happen 100% locally inside your browser on HTML5 Canvas.
