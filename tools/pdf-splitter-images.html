Complete Guide to PDF Splitting & Image Compilation

Managing large PDF documents frequently requires isolating specific sections—such as separating legal addendums, pulling out an invoice page from a 100-page bank record, or compiling digital artwork portfolios into single downloadable books. MultiTools Hub pairs a precision PDF page extractor with a versatile image-to-PDF compiler, all functioning completely client-side.

How to Extract Exact Page Subsets or Individual Zips

Our custom range syntax gives you granular control over exactly which pages survive into the final document:

  • Continuous Spans: Type 1-5 to extract the first five pages.
  • Selective Pages: Type 1, 4, 9 to pull non-contiguous pages.
  • Complex Combinations: Type 1-3, 7, 12-15 to extract mixed intervals in one click.
  • Bulk Extraction: Use the "Extract All Pages (ZIP)" feature to instantly generate a portable ZIP archive where each page is saved as its own standalone PDF file.

Converting Multi-Format Images into Archival PDFs

Freelancers and creators often receive assets in mixed formats: some in PNG with transparent backgrounds, high-resolution scans in JPG, and modern web graphics in WebP. Combining them conventionally usually requires expensive desktop software.

MultiTools Hub automatically processes transparency on HTML5 canvas with a clean white backdrop, normalizes image aspect ratios, and embeds them into standardized PDF dimensions (A4, US Letter, or native pixel sizing). Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does custom page range splitting work?

You can enter individual pages and page spans separated by commas, such as '1-3, 5, 8-10'. The browser tool extracts only those exact pages into a brand new PDF document.

Can I extract every single page into separate PDF files?

Yes! Clicking 'Extract All Pages to ZIP' splits each page into its own individual PDF (e.g., page-001.pdf, page-002.pdf) and archives them into a downloadable ZIP file.

What image formats are supported in the Images-to-PDF converter?

JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP images are supported. Images can be arranged in custom sequences and formatted into standard A4, US Letter, or Fit-to-Image page dimensions.

Are my images or PDF files uploaded to an external server?

Never. All parsing, slicing, canvas rendering, and ZIP compression occur 100% client-side in your web browser session.
